Client Demo — Presentation Script

⚓ PalLite PMS Demo

⏱ ~25 min total 7 sections
Your opening line — say this first
"PalLite is a fleet maintenance platform built specifically for how your operations team actually works — everything from vessel health to job orders to spare parts, all connected to a single live database."

Then pause. Let it land. Don't rush into features immediately.

Prep

2
Fleet Dashboard
4 min
Say
"The first thing after login is the Fleet Dashboard. Designed to answer one question: what needs my attention right now?"
Show
Point to the five stat cards: Total Vessels, At Sea, In Port, In Maintenance, Critical Defects. "These update in real time from Zoho CRM."
Say
"The Fleet Map shows where every vessel is right now — in port, at sea, in maintenance — colour coded. Click any pin to pull up that vessel."
Show
Point to the Fleet Health Score donut chart. "A single score for the entire fleet based on open defects and pending maintenance. You want this number high."
Show
Scroll to the Maintenance Heatmap and Jobs Completed trend. "These two charts together tell you if your maintenance programme is keeping up or falling behind."
If asked
"What does the score mean?" → "It's a weighted composite of open critical defects, overdue jobs, and vessels in maintenance. We can tune the weighting to match your KPIs."

4
Maintenance Workflow
5 min
Say
"Let me walk you through how a piece of maintenance work flows through the system from plan to completion."
Click
Click Due Jobs in the sidebar.
Say
"Due Jobs shows everything overdue or coming up for maintenance across the fleet. Colour-coded by urgency — critical overdue items are immediately visible."
Click
Click Job Orders.
Say
"When a job needs to be executed, it becomes a Job Order. Assigned to a technician, tracked through Open → In Progress → Completed, and requires approval before closing."
Click
Click Defects.
Say
"Defects can be reported by any crew member. Categorised by severity, assigned to a vessel and component, tracked until resolved. Nothing falls through the cracks."
Click
Click Approvals.
Say
"All job completions require approval. Full audit trail of every decision. The Chief Engineer or Fleet Administrator reviews, can request changes, and signs off."
If asked
"Can we get notifications?" → "Email alerts are configured per event type. A technician gets notified when a job is assigned, the CE gets notified when approval is needed."
5
MDM & Administration
5 min
Say
"Now the admin side — this is what your system administrator controls. Most users never see this area."
Click
Click Common under MDM in the sidebar.
Say
"Master Data Management — all reference data lives here. Company types, vessel types, flags, ports, currencies, countries. Every dropdown in the system draws from here. Your data stays consistent."
Show
Click through a couple of sub-items — All Companies, then Flags. Show the Add button and search bar. "Full add, edit, delete, search. All changes sync to Zoho CRM instantly."
Click
Click Administration in the sidebar.
Say
"Administration shows all active Zoho CRM users — name, email, role, profile — pulled live from the system. User management happens in Zoho, keeping a single source of truth."
If asked
"Can we add users from here?" → "User creation is handled in Zoho CRM admin to maintain security governance. This view gives your team visibility without giving everyone access to user provisioning."
6
Q&A + Closing
4 min
Where is the data stored?
Zoho CRM — your existing Zoho account. No new server, no new database to maintain.
Can we use it on mobile?
The web app is responsive and works in any browser on any device. A native mobile app is on the roadmap.
What if Zoho is down?
The app relies on the Zoho CRM API. Zoho maintains 99.9% uptime SLA — the same reliability your CRM runs on today.
Can we import our existing data?
Yes — data can be bulk-imported into Zoho CRM via CSV, then it immediately appears in PalLite.
How many vessels can it handle?
There is no hard limit — it scales with Zoho CRM's capacity. Deployments with 50+ vessels are supported.
Who controls who sees what?
Three built-in roles: Fleet Administrator, Chief Engineer, Technician. Roles are managed in Zoho and control which modules and actions each user can access.
What does customisation cost?
The MDM section means most reference data — company types, vessel classes, port lists — is fully configurable by your admin without any code changes.
Is the code ours?
Yes — the source code is in your Git repository and fully owned by you.
